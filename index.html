About this reconstruction

This is a speculative reconstruction. Today's terrain and imagery are real. The maar, tuff ring, cinder cone and lava lake are modelled from the outline of today's basalt cap. The age (about 3.5 million years) and the eruption sequence are simplified, and fine detail below the data resolution is generated.

The time column runs from the oldest stage at the bottom to today at the top. The eruption is stretched out so you can watch it; the wavy lines mark where the time scale changes.
